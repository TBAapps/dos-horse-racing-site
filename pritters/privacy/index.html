Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy covers the Pritters mobile game, published by Timbuk-Also LLC ("we," "us"). The short version: Pritters does not collect your personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Pritters has no accounts and no login, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. We do not use analytics or tracking tools, and the game contains no advertising.

What stays on your device

Your game progress is saved only on your own device. That includes the Pritters you've caught, your bait and power-ups, your level in each mini game, your cast statistics, and your sound settings. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it, and you can also clear it at any time with Reset Game in the app's menu.

Third parties

Pritters does not share data with third parties, because it does not collect any. The game does not contain ads, and it does not use third-party advertising or analytics services. Apple (or Google) may collect standard information about downloads and crash reports according to their own privacy policies and your device settings, and we do not receive personally identifiable information from them.

Purchases

The game does not currently contain in-app purchases. If that ever changes, any purchase would be handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play, and we would never see or store your payment information. This policy would be updated first.

Children's privacy

Because Pritters collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. We do not knowingly collect information from users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to admin@timbukalso.com.
