Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy covers the Pritters mobile game, published by Timbuk-Also LLC ("we," "us"). The short version: Pritters has no accounts and we don't collect your name, email, or other personal details. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and those ads may use your device's advertising identifier.

What we collect

We don't collect personal information ourselves. Pritters has no accounts or login, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

What stays on your device

Your game progress is saved only on your own device. That includes the Pritters you've caught (including holiday Pritters), your trophies, your bait and power-ups, your level in each mini game, your cast statistics, and your sound settings. This information is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it, and you can also clear it at any time with Reset Game in the app's menu.

Advertising

Pritters is free to play and shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads only appear when you choose to watch one, for example to earn bonus bait or double a mini game prize. (Version 1.0.0 also showed full-screen ads between mini games; these were removed in version 1.0.1.) To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, your approximate location (from your IP address), information about the ads you've seen and interacted with, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information. Google may use this data to show you ads and to measure how well ads perform. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your tracking choice

On iPhone, Pritters asks for your permission before it uses your advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you say no, you can still play everything in the game. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Ads will still appear, but they may be less relevant to you.

Users in the EU, EEA and UK

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, Pritters shows a consent form from Google (its User Messaging Platform) the first time you open the game. The form asks whether Google and its ad partners may use cookies and device identifiers to show you personalized ads and measure ads. You can choose not to consent. If you do not consent, you can still play everything in the game and you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. Your choice is stored on your device. If you have questions about your choice, contact us at the address below.

Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases: the Golden Lure and the Small and Large Tackle Box (bags of bait). Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information. If you bought Remove Ads in version 1.0.0, it no longer has anything to remove, because version 1.0.1 shows no full-screen ads to anyone.

Children's privacy

Pritters is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Ads are served by Google under its own policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to admin@timbukalso.com.
