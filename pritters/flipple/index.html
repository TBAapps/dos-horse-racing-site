Flipple

Common Fanwart Ponds

An acrobatic jumping fish often seen doing flips and making big splashes in the water.

Where to find it
Fanwart Ponds
Rarity
Common. Common Pritters bite often, even on a missed cast.
Secret forms
An Underworld and an Auroral form, which start appearing once you've caught all 20 Pritters.
Trophies
Catch 15, 25 and 35 Flipples (any form) and Elder Harry carves it in wood, stone and gold.
