Shrimpington

Uncommon Fanwart Ponds

An extra-fancy-looking shrimp due to its black circle around one eye and nearly tuxedo-like markings on its body.

Where to find it
Fanwart Ponds
Rarity
Uncommon. Better casts make it more likely.
Secret forms
An Underworld and an Auroral form, which start appearing once you've caught all 20 Pritters.
Trophies
Catch 15, 25 and 35 Shrimpingtons (any form) and Elder Harry carves it in wood, stone and gold.
