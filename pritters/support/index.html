Common questions

I'm out of Bait Berries. What now?

Open the menu and choose Get Bait (or tap the berry counter), then win any of the four mini games to earn 3 Bait Berries.

How do I see the tutorial again?

Open the menu and tap Replay Tutorial. The tour will walk you through everything again. The Guide in the same menu has the rules written down.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. Pritters has no accounts or cloud saving yet, so deleting the app or switching to a new phone starts you fresh.

How do I start over?

Open the menu and tap Reset Game. This erases your collection and progress, and it can't be undone.

Does Pritters need an internet connection?

Playing doesn't. Your catches, bait, and progress all live on your device. You only need a connection to watch ads, make a purchase, or restore a purchase.

Does Pritters have ads or in-app purchases?

Yes. Pritters is free to play. It shows a full-screen ad after every second mini game, and you can choose to watch an optional ad for bonus bait. There are two optional purchases, Remove Ads and Golden Lure. You can find them by tapping the gold Perks button in the menu.

What do Remove Ads and Golden Lure do?

Remove Ads stops the full-screen ads between mini games. The optional ads that give bonus bait stay, if you want them. Golden Lure guarantees your next cast is a Pritter you haven't caught yet, including Underworld and Auroral forms once they unlock. A Golden Lure is used up when it works, so you can buy more whenever you like.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the menu, tap Perks, then tap Restore Purchases. This brings back Remove Ads on a new phone or after reinstalling the app. You need to be signed in to the same Apple ID you bought it with. Golden Lures that you haven't used yet can't be restored, so use them before you switch phones.

How do I change my ad tracking choice?

On iPhone, go to Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking, and switch Pritters on or off. You'll still see ads either way, but they may be less relevant if tracking is off.
