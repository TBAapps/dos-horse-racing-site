Common questions

I'm out of Bait Berries. What now?

Tap Get Bait (or tap the berry count under the pond name). Win any of the four mini games to earn 3 Bait Berries, or watch a short optional ad for 3 more. If you'd rather skip the games, Tackle Boxes are optional bait packs at the top of the same screen.

How do I see the tutorial again?

Open the menu and tap Replay Tutorial. The tour will walk you through everything again. The Guide in the same menu has the rules written down.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. Pritters has no accounts or cloud saving yet, so deleting the app or switching to a new phone starts you fresh.

Where are my trophies?

Tap the trophy button on the left side of the pond screen (or in the top corner of your Collection) to visit your trophy room. Elder Harry carves a wooden trophy when you've caught 15 of one Pritter, stone at 25, and gold at 35.

Why are some Pritters wearing Santa hats?

During Christmas week (December 18 to 25), some of your catches show up dressed for the season. They get their own Christmas page in your Collection.

How do I start over?

Open the menu and tap Reset Game. This erases your collection and progress, and it can't be undone.

Does Pritters need an internet connection?

Playing doesn't. Your catches, bait, and progress all live on your device. You only need a connection to watch ads, make a purchase, or restore a purchase.

Does Pritters have ads or in-app purchases?

Pritters is free to play. There are no pop-up ads. The only ads are ones you choose to watch for bonus bait. There are three optional purchases: the Golden Lure and two Tackle Boxes. You'll find them all under the gold Perks button in the menu, and the Tackle Boxes are also on the Get Bait screen.

What do the Golden Lure and Tackle Boxes do?

A Golden Lure guarantees your next catch is a Pritter you haven't caught yet, including secret forms once they unlock. Tackle Boxes add Bait Berries right away: 30 in the Small box and 120 in the Large box. Each one is used up when you use it, so you can buy more whenever you like.

A purchase didn't show up. What do I do?

Open the menu, tap Perks, then tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple ID you bought it with. Golden Lures and Bait Berries live on the phone you bought them on, so use them up before you switch phones or delete the app.

How do I change my ad tracking choice?

On iPhone, go to Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking, and switch Pritters on or off. You'll still see ads either way, but they may be less relevant if tracking is off.
