About the game

Short description: Pritters is a cozy fishing and collecting game for iPhone. Cast your line into Fanwart Ponds, time your taps, and catch odd little critters called Pritters.

Longer description: You've just moved to Fanwart Village, where everybody fishes, but what they catch aren't fish at all. They're Pritters: twenty strange, lovable critters living in Fanwart Ponds. Every cast is a quick timing game, and the better your cast, the rarer your catch. Catch all twenty to unlock their secret Underworld and Auroral forms, earn trophies carved by Elder Harry, and win quick mini games for the Bait Berries that keep you fishing. No accounts, nothing to sign up for, and it plays offline.

Features

  • 20 Pritters to catch across five rarities, plus 40 secret forms
  • A skill-based cast: three timed stops decide how rare your catch is
  • Wood, stone and gold trophies for every Pritter, shown in your own trophy room
  • Four mini games for bait: Berry Match, Basket Catch, Image Shuffle and Memory Match
  • Power-ups earned by casting, a region map to explore, and holiday Pritters
  • Coming in 2027: Pritter Days, New Pritter Sundays and new areas of Fanwart
