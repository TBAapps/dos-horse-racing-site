Deweaver

Common Fanwart Ponds

A cute little water spider that lays its eggs in the dew drops that form on its webs hanging over the water. When the droplets get full, they fall into the pond and sink to the bottom, where they are protected until the babies are born.

Where to find it
Fanwart Ponds
Rarity
Common. Common Pritters bite often, even on a missed cast.
Secret forms
An Underworld and an Auroral form, which start appearing once you've caught all 20 Pritters.
Trophies
Catch 15, 25 and 35 Deweavers (any form) and Elder Harry carves it in wood, stone and gold.
