What is Pritters?

Pritters is a cozy, offline collecting game. You've just moved to a village where everybody fishes, and what they catch aren't fish at all. They're Pritters: twenty strange, lovable critters that live in Fanwart Ponds.

Every cast is a little skill game, every catch goes into your collection, and a quick walkthrough shows you the ropes. No accounts, no ads, and nothing to sign up for. Just you, a fishing rod, and a pond.

How it plays

1

Cast

Stop the sliding marker inside three glowing targets. Land more of them and rarer critters bite.

2

Collect

Every catch joins your collection. Catch them all to complete the pond.

3

Earn bait

Each cast costs a Bait Berry. Win mini games (Berry Match, Basket Catch, Image Shuffle and Memory Match) to earn more.

4

Power up

Every cast charges your battery. Fill it to earn tools that widen targets, slow the marker, and more.
