What is Pritters?

Pritters is a cozy collecting game. You've just moved to a village where everybody fishes, and what they catch aren't fish at all. They're Pritters: twenty strange, lovable critters that live in Fanwart Ponds.

Every cast is a little skill game, every catch goes into your collection, and Elder Harry shows you the ropes. Keep catching your favorites and he'll carve you trophies for your very own trophy room. Rumor has it some Pritters have secret forms, too.

No accounts and nothing to sign up for. It's free to play, with optional ads you choose to watch and optional purchases. Just you, a fishing rod, and a pond.

How it plays

1

Cast

Stop the sliding marker inside three glowing targets. Land more of them and rarer critters bite.

2

Collect

Every catch joins your collection. Catch them all to complete the pond.

3

Earn bait

Each cast costs a Bait Berry. Win quick mini games (Berry Match, Basket Catch, Image Shuffle and Memory Match) to earn more.

4

Power up

Every cast charges your battery. Fill it to earn tools that widen targets, slow the marker, and more.

5

Win trophies

Catch 15, 25 and 35 of a Pritter and Elder Harry carves it in wood, stone and gold for your trophy room.
