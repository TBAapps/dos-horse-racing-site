Pritters Events & Schedule 2027

Events come to the Party Pool, a new spot on the map, in the next Pritters update. Here's the 2027 calendar so you can plan ahead. Events run midnight to midnight, Pacific time.

Pritter Days

Every third Tuesday, one Pritter is featured at the Party Pool. It bites far more often there, and catching it earns prizes: Bait Berries, a free Event Lure, and an ultra-rare event version that earns a trading card for the binder in your trophy room.

Holiday weeks

During each holiday week, some of your everyday catches show up dressed for the occasion, and each holiday gets its own page in your Collection. Holiday weeks run from 7 days before the holiday through the day itself. Christmas week is already in the game: this year it runs December 18 to 25, 2026.

Missed one?

Joined late? Missed events come back for you on catch-up Thursdays, oldest first, so new players can still earn every prize.
