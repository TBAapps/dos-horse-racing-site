Mermatee

Epic Fanwart Ponds

Sailor lore talks about tales of mermaids; other people assume they only saw manatees. Neither may be wrong...

Where to find it
Fanwart Ponds
Rarity
Epic. Better casts make it more likely.
Secret forms
An Underworld and an Auroral form, which start appearing once you've caught all 20 Pritters.
Trophies
Catch 15, 25 and 35 Mermatees (any form) and Elder Harry carves it in wood, stone and gold.
