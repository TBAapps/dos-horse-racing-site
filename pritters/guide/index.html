How to Play Pritters: Walkthrough, Hints & Tips

"New to the village? Everything you need to know is right here. And if you forget, the Guide in the game's menu has it all written down too."
Elder Harry

1. Your fishing screen

  • Collection (top left): every Pritter you've caught.
  • Trophies: your trophy room.
  • Bait Berries (under the pond name): how many casts you have. Tap it to get more.
  • Battery and power-up slots (right): your battery fills as you cast, and power-ups wait in the slots.
  • Golden Lure: tap it before a cast to guarantee a Pritter you haven't caught yet.

2. Casting

Tap CAST. A marker sweeps across the timing bar, and you tap STOP three times: in the left target, the right target, then the center. A green check or red X pops up above the bar where each stop landed. The more targets you hit, the rarer your catch can be.

A missed cast can also snag junk. Junk washes away the power-ups you're holding and empties your battery, so try to land at least two targets.

3. Bait Berries

Every cast uses one Bait Berry. When you run out, the Cast button becomes Get Bait. Win any of the four mini games (Berry Match, Basket Catch, Image Shuffle, Memory Match) for 3 berries, or watch a short optional ad for 3 more. You can also watch an ad to double a mini game prize.

Rather skip the games? Tackle Boxes are optional bait packs: 30 berries in the Small box, 120 in the Large box.

4. Battery & power-ups

Every cast charges your battery by one, and a Perfect cast charges it by two. When it fills up (6 charges), you earn a power-up. You can hold two at a time and tap one to use it on your next cast.

Slower Marker

The marker sweeps twice as slowly on every stage of your next cast, so it is easier to stop in the targets.

Bigger Target

The target zones on your next cast are twice as wide.

Zero-out Common

Your next cast can't catch a Common, so Uncommon and rarer Pritters become more likely.

Double Cast

Your next cast catches two Pritters, and the second is always rarer than the first.

Second Chance

If your catch isn't Legendary, you can swap it for a hidden upgrade to a higher rarity.

Variant Surge

After the secret forms unlock: your next catch is guaranteed to be a secret form.

Dark Energy

After the secret forms unlock: your next catch is guaranteed to be an Underworld form.

Starlight Beam

After the secret forms unlock: your next catch is guaranteed to be an Auroral form.

5. Collection & secret forms

Twenty Pritters live in Fanwart Ponds. Catch all twenty and something odd starts happening: each Pritter can now show up in a spooky Underworld form or a glowing Auroral form. That's 60 forms to collect. See all 20 Pritters.

6. Trophies

Every form of a Pritter counts toward its trophies. Tap the Trophies button to see your shelves.

Hints & tips

  • Watch the rhythm, not the marker. The sweep is steady, so tap a beat early as it closes in on each target.
  • Save Bigger Target and Slower Marker for when you're chasing a Legendary. They make a Perfect cast much easier.
  • Perfect casts charge your battery twice as fast, so good aim pays off twice.
  • Memory Match has no timer. It's the most relaxed way to earn bait.
  • The last few secret forms are the rarest. That's the best time to use a Golden Lure.
  • Keep catching your favorites after you've found them. Every catch counts toward trophies.
  • Fish during Christmas week (December 18 to 25) to find Pritters in Santa hats.

Quick answers

How do I catch rare Pritters?

Land more targets. A Perfect cast (all three targets) gives a 10% chance at a Legendary, while a missed cast almost always brings a Common.

How do I get more Bait Berries?

Win any of the four mini games for 3 berries, watch an optional ad for 3, double a mini game prize with an ad, or buy a Tackle Box.

How do I unlock the secret forms?

Catch all 20 Pritters in Fanwart Ponds. After that, each Pritter can also appear in its Underworld or Auroral form.

How do trophies work?

Catch 15, 25 and 35 of the same Pritter, counting all its forms, to earn its wood, stone and gold trophy.
