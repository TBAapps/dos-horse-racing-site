Squecho

Rare Fanwart Ponds

An extremely friendly dolphin that is known to play with all humans and has shown up to help people in distress.

Where to find it
Fanwart Ponds
Rarity
Rare. Better casts make it more likely.
Secret forms
An Underworld and an Auroral form, which start appearing once you've caught all 20 Pritters.
Trophies
Catch 15, 25 and 35 Squechos (any form) and Elder Harry carves it in wood, stone and gold.
