Plotter

Common Fanwart Ponds

A round, fat otter who loves to chow down on any food he can find. Young ones will sometimes steal the lunchbox from fishermen, but older ones get too big and slow to even try.

Where to find it
Fanwart Ponds
Rarity
Common. Common Pritters bite often, even on a missed cast.
Secret forms
An Underworld and an Auroral form, which start appearing once you've caught all 20 Pritters.
Trophies
Catch 15, 25 and 35 Plotters (any form) and Elder Harry carves it in wood, stone and gold.
